Hair and cloth drift left. Entire sprite including head and feet within frame with padding, transparent background, no shadow no text.

## 动作帧生成提示词（内置 ImageGen）

Create a production animation sprite atlas of EXACTLY EIGHT separate full-body poses of the reference white-haired chibi witch with crimson and black hanfu, large black ribbon, little crescent staff. Four columns by two rows uniform grid on transparent canvas, landscape 2:1, each pose entirely within its own square cell with transparent gutters. Cute lively anime pixel art, consistent proportions, colors and scale. All face screen right in 3/4 profile. Top row left to right: running stride front leg extended; running opposite stride / trailing boot high; diving horizontally head first, arms forward, feet trailing airborne; upside-down tightly curled somersault with boots above head. Bottom row left to right: kneeling on one knee pushing up after landing; spell anticipation drawing staff behind head; forceful spell release pointing crescent staff forward; relaxed follow through with sleeve and hair settling. No VFX, no ground, no text, no cell outlines. Keep clear air between all eight individual figures, especially adjacent staff tips. Each frame single character only.

## 选关地图与太虚灵田

地点贴纸为 assets/field-pin.png 和 assets/castle-pin.png，两枚 72 × 72 透明像素图由内置 ImageGen 生成后分切。地图的地形分层、河流、路径和交互标记由 journey.js 与 journey.css 绘制。灵田的土壤、麦穗、风和夕阳使用 field.js 的几何与 shader。家用稻草人为草帽、补丁布袍、草束及镰刀组成的分节模型。

A tiny 16-bit game location icon sprite sheet with exactly TWO icons side by side, equally spaced in left and right square cells, generous transparent gap. Reference witch sprite is STYLE ONLY: match its restrained dark maroon outline, soft muted flat cel colors, very cute chibi chunky proportions. No character. Draw these as if only 48 by 48 logical pixels per icon, hard visible pixel clusters, 8 colors per icon, no texture, no gradient, no detailed architecture, no lettering, no ornate enamel or white sticker rim. Left icon: ONE tiny Chinese farm pavilion with one red-brown roof, golden wheat tuft and straw hat on stake on small green patch. Right icon: squat violet-black mini castle with two towers, one dark arched gate, one red banner and one orange brazier. Compact cheerful map stickers. BIG simple shapes, readable from far. A clean transparent background only. Landscape 2:1, detached from each other, both whole and centered in own cell.
